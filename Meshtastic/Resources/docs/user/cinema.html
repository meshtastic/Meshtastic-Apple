Cinema (Apple Vision Pro)

On Apple Vision Pro, the Cinema puts the app on a big screen inside a search and rescue comms tent, a ham shack or a fire lookout. Open it with the tent button in the round glass menu under the app's tab bar. The app's window closes while you're in the tent and comes back when you leave.

The screen

The screen on the tent's front wall shows the app. The tabs above it are the app's own: Messages, Nodes, Map, Settings and Connect. It opens on Connect. Sheets, menus and dialogs that would pop up over the app open as pages beside the screen instead. Things that only work in the app's window, like sharing or saving a file, show an Open in App Window button in their place. While the radio is locked down or needs a firmware update, the screen says to continue in the app window.

The 3D map

The 3D spatial map opens in front of you when you enter, at its largest size. If you close it, Open 3D Map on the control panel brings it back.

Radio models

On a node's details, the AR button on the radio's 3D model stands the model on a clear stand in front of you, at real size. Pinch it to pick it up and turn it over; let go and it goes back on the stand. The card beside it shows the node's name, when it was last heard, its battery, signal and hops. Pinch the bar under the stand to move it, and the X on the card puts the model away. Outside the tent, the AR button places the model in the room around you instead.

Boards

  • Team Favorites, on the whiteboard: your favorite nodes and the connected radio, with when each was last heard, its battery, signal, hops and distance from the radio. Tap a node to center the 3D map on it and open its details on the screen.
  • The latest trace route, on the framed print (by the door in the tent, beside the window in the ham shack): the same card the trace route widget shows, for the last trace route you sent or heard. Tap it to show the route on the screen's map and fly it on the 3D map.
  • Status Board, on the portrait whiteboard beside the screen: your radio's own status message, then the nodes heard with one, latest first, as many as fit, with a count of the rest. Tap a node to center the 3D map on it and open its details on the screen. Set your radio's status from its node details.

Fire lookout

The third place is a fire lookout: a glass cab on a tall tower on a summit at sunset, looking out over forested ridges, with its storm shutters propped open as awnings all round, the screen hanging in the front windows, the team whiteboard in a bay on the left and the trace route print in one on the right, with the view all round them.

If you've downloaded terrain for the 3D map, the view out the windows can be the real thing. The control panel's Summit picker lists up to ten of the highest mountains in the downloaded area, highest first, with how far each is from your radio and which way. Pick one and the cab stands on it, with the terrain around it out every window, lit by the same sunset. Every node with a position is marked out the windows where it really is from there, by bearing and distance: its round chip on a short stem over the spot. Nodes too close together to tell apart share one marker, with how many there are on it. A node behind the screen or a board is out of sight.

Tap a marker to open its card beside it, so you don't have to turn to the screen: the node's name, how far away it is and its bearing, its altitude, when it was last heard, its battery, signal and hops. Show on Map centers the 3D map on the node, and Show on Screen opens its details on the screen. A marker that stands for several nodes lists them first; pick one to see its card. Tap the marker again, or the card's close button, to close it. Forest sunset puts the forest view back.

While the cab stands on a summit, the 3D map shows the lookout there too: the cab on its braced timber tower, facing the way the screen does. If a node stands on the same summit, the lookout stands just beside it.

Control panel

  • Environment: the place the screen is set in: the search and rescue tent, or a ham shack, a wood-paneled radio room with an operating desk, a window out to the antenna tower, and the same boards; its status board is net control's check-in board, standing portrait beside the screen; or a fire lookout, a glass cab on a summit at sunset.
  • Summit: in the fire lookout, with terrain downloaded, which summit the cab stands on, or the forest sunset.
  • Turn Lookout: with a summit picked, a compass dial for which way the cab faces, so the screen looks out over another part of the view. Tap one of its eight marks, an eighth of a turn (45°) apart, or drag round it and let go. The caption under it says which way the cab faces. The cab on the 3D map turns with it.
  • Open 3D Map: shown while the 3D map is closed.
  • Move it: pinch the bar under the panel and drag it wherever you want it.
  • Leave: closes the tent and brings back the app's window.

The tent also closes when you leave the app.
